Getting here and around

Cruise ship Air

From the cruise dock, Yellow Leaf Bay

Short walk or bus into Taniti City. The ship docks one night per week, so check your visit date.

From the airport

The airport is small and takes small jets and propeller planes. Collect rental cars from the agency near the airport, or take a taxi into Taniti City.
