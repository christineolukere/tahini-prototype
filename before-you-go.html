Before you go

Money and currency

U.S. dollars; banks exchange euros and yen; cards widely accepted.

Holidays and hours

Taniti has many national holidays. Many attractions and restaurants close or shorten their hours, so check your dates.

Safety and health

Violent crime is rare; watch for pickpockets in busy areas. One hospital with multilingual staff, plus several clinics.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak English. Little English is spoken in rural areas.

Power and local rules

120 volts, U.S. outlets. No alcohol sold from midnight to 9 a.m. Bike helmets required by law.
